Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Summary

Pixel Postmortem Publisher (the "App") is used by the Pixel Postmortem team (the "Operator") to publish its own videos to its own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It has no public users, collects no data about viewers or other TikTok users, shows no ads and sells no data.

2. Data the App handles

  • OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) issued by TikTok when the account owner authorizes the App. They are stored in a private file readable only by the Operator on the Operator's own server, and are used only to call the TikTok APIs on behalf of that account.
  • Basic account information (user.info.basic: display name, username, avatar) and creator posting settings returned by TikTok (available privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video duration). They are displayed on the confirmation screen before posting and are not stored beyond what is needed for that screen.
  • Publishing records: the post / publish identifier and status returned by TikTok for each video the Operator publishes, kept in the Operator's local database to track its own uploads.
  • The videos and captions that the Operator itself produces and chooses to publish.

3. How the data is used

Only to authenticate the account owner, display who will receive the post, upload and publish the Operator's videos, and check the publishing status. The data is not used for profiling, advertising or any other purpose.

4. Sharing

Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (TikTok's APIs for TikTok data). It is not sold, rented or shared with any third party.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the App is authorized. When the account owner revokes access (TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or stops using the App, the Operator deletes the stored tokens. Publishing records are kept as long as the Operator keeps the related videos.

6. Security

Secrets are stored outside the source code, with file permissions restricted to the Operator, on a server reachable only through an encrypted connection. All API calls use HTTPS.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: contact us via the Pixel Postmortem channel on TikTok or YouTube.
