Terms of Service

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. The service

Pixel Postmortem Publisher (the "App") is a publishing tool operated by the Pixel Postmortem team (the "Operator"). It renders short videos about video-game history and publishes them to the Pixel Postmortem channel's own accounts on TikTok and YouTube through the platforms' official APIs.

2. Who may use the App

The App is used only by the Operator, to manage the Operator's own channel accounts. There is no public sign-up and no third-party user accounts. By connecting a TikTok account to the App, the account owner confirms that they own or are authorized to manage that account.

3. Platform terms

Use of the App with TikTok is subject to TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Music Usage Confirmation, and, for commercial content, TikTok's Branded Content Policy. Every post is reviewed and explicitly confirmed by the account owner before it is sent to TikTok.

4. Content

The Operator is responsible for the videos it publishes, including captions, privacy settings, interaction settings, commercial content disclosure and the AI-generated content label. The App does not publish anything without the account owner's confirmation.

5. Availability and changes

The App is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. The Operator may change, suspend or stop the App at any time, and may update these Terms; the date above shows the latest version.

6. Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the App's access at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Stored tokens are then deleted (see the Privacy Policy).

7. Contact

Contact us via the Pixel Postmortem channel on TikTok or YouTube.
