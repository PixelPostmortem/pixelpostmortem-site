The publishing tool of the Pixel Postmortem channel

Pixel Postmortem is a short-form video channel about video-game history: failed consoles, cancelled games and the stories behind them. Pixel Postmortem Publisher is the tool its operator uses to render those videos and publish them to the channel's own accounts through official platform APIs.

What the app does

  1. Produce — a fact-checked script is turned into a vertical 1080×1920 video (narration, subtitles, archive images) on the operator's own server.
  2. Review — the operator watches the rendered master and approves it.
  3. Publish — the operator publishes the approved video to the channel's accounts (TikTok, YouTube) using the platforms' official APIs.

TikTok integration

The app uses Login Kit and the Content Posting API with the user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish scopes, only for the channel's own TikTok account (@pixelpostmortem):

  • The account owner signs in with TikTok and authorizes the app (OAuth 2.0 with PKCE).
  • Before each post, the app queries the latest creator info and shows a confirmation screen with the creator's nickname, the caption, the AI-generated content label, a privacy level chosen manually among the options returned by TikTok (no default), the comment / duet / stitch settings (off by default), the commercial content disclosure and the Music Usage Confirmation consent.
  • Nothing is uploaded until the operator explicitly confirms. Videos can also be sent to the TikTok inbox as drafts, to be finished and posted from the TikTok app.
  • Videos are labeled as AI-generated content when synthetic narration or images are used.

Who can use it

Pixel Postmortem Publisher is operated by the Pixel Postmortem team for the Pixel Postmortem channel. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not access any other TikTok user's data.
